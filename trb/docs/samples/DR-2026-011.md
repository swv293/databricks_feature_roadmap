# Decision Record DR-2026-011: Audit logs

**Status:** Approved · **Approved scope:** PHI · **Decided at:** TRBM-2026-05-05
**Review:** TRB-2026-011 · **Feature:** `Audit logs@Platform & Governance` · **Databricks phase at decision:** GA

## Context
Information Security presented UC audit logs as the immutable evidence source for HIPAA and SOC 2 audits. Retention, tamper-proof storage, and query performance were evaluated.

## Decision
Audit logs are approved as the payer's audit source. Retention follows the enterprise records-retention policy (7 years), implemented by the platform team through audit-log delivery and system-table exports; all production workspaces must deliver audit logs.

## Risks considered
- Log volume for high-scale environments
- Query complexity for forensics
- Long-term retention cost

## Mitigations relied on
- Audit logs stored in managed UC tables with VACUUM restrictions
- System tables provide efficient log queries
- Tiered retention policy: hot 90 days, cold 7 years

## Control mappings
- HIPAA-Security-Rule 164.312(b) (Audit controls): Satisfied
- NIST-800-53-R5 AU-2 (Audit Events): Satisfied
- HITRUST-CSF 02.e (Protection of Audit Information): Satisfied
