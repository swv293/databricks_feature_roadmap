**Review ID:** TRB-2026-017 · **Feature:** `Lakebase (Postgres OLTP)@Storage & Formats` · **Databricks phase at submission:** GA
**Requesting business unit:** Member Experience · **Submitted:** 2026-09-19 · **Scheduled for:** TRBM-2026-10-06

## Business need
Member Experience submitted Lakebase (managed Postgres OLTP) for powering member-facing apps with low-latency serving next to the lakehouse. TRB is reviewing backup/recovery, retention, and PHI-at-rest encryption.

## Intended use cases
- Member portal app backing store
- Low-latency care-team reference data
- Operational app transactions

## Risks identified by the requester
- Automatic backups may retain deleted PHI
- Retention policy not aligned to HIPAA 6-year minimum
- CMK coverage for Postgres storage

## Proposed mitigations
- Backup retention tuned to 7-year hold period
- Automated backup lifecycle per retention policy
- CMK applied to all Postgres instances
