# Review Intake Request: Clean Rooms

**Review ID:** TRB-2026-024 · **Feature:** `Clean Rooms@Sharing & Collaboration` · **Databricks phase at submission:** GA
**Requesting business unit:** Provider Strategy · **Submitted:** 2026-09-23 · **Scheduled for:** TRBM-2026-10-06
**Primary reviewer:** Chief Privacy Officer

## Business need
Provider Strategy submitted Clean Rooms for joint analytics with provider networks where neither party exposes raw PHI. TRB is reviewing PHI handling in multi-party outputs and approval workflows.

## Intended use cases
- Payer-provider joint analytics (de-identified)
- Network analysis without data exchange
- Quality improvement collaborative projects

## Risks identified by the requester
- De-identification logic insufficient for re-identification risk
- Query results leak member-level PHI through aggregates
- Collaborator dataset inadvertently contains PII

## Proposed mitigations
- De-identification rules reviewed by Chief Privacy Officer per-engagement
- Output tables limited to summary statistics (counts, rates)
- Collaborator dataset scan for PII before upload

- Databricks documentation: https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/clean-rooms/
- Known limitations (tracker): Max 10 collaborators; serverless workspace required.
