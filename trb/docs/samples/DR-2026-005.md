# Architectural Decision Record (ADR) – TRB Decision: Genie One

**Decision Record ID:** DR-2026-005  
**Title:** Approve Genie One (SQL Query Generator) for PHI-Containing Data  
**Related Review:** TRB-2026-005 (Genie One – SQL & BI Analytics)  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** July 28, 2026  

---

## 1. Context & Problem Statement

The Analytics team at Acme Health Plan requested approval to use Databricks Genie One for member data exploration and ad-hoc analytics queries. Genie One is a natural-language query generator (GA feature in Databricks). The team wants to use it for member claims analysis, enabling non-technical stakeholders to ask questions in English rather than writing SQL.

**The Decision:**
- Feature is GA and functionally sound
- Risk is centered on PHI disclosure in prompts and low-count aggregates in responses
- TRB needed to determine: Can this feature be safely used with member PHI?

---

## 2. Decision

**APPROVED FOR USE WITH MEMBER PHI, CONTINGENT ON CONDITIONS**

Genie One is approved for use by authorized analytics users when executing queries against member data (demographics, claims, coverage). Feature is PHI-eligible once conditions are met and verified.

---

## 3. Alternative Decisions Considered

**Option A: Unconditional Approval**
- Rationale: Feature is GA; Databricks has mature security controls.
- Risk: No audit control on natural-language prompts. Users could disclose PHI in queries (e.g., "Show me John Doe's claims"). LLM responses could reveal low-count cohorts (privacy attack).
- Decision: Rejected. Insufficient audit and monitoring.

**Option B: Reject (Non-PHI Only)**
- Rationale: Genie prompts could leak PHI; low-count suppression not built-in; too risky for HIPAA data.
- Risk: Blocks valuable analytic capability. Undermines governance (appears over-cautious).
- Decision: Rejected. Conditions can mitigate to acceptable level.

**Option C: Conditional Approval** ← **SELECTED**
- Rationale: Strong audit logging (captures prompts), response sampling (catches low-count issues), user training, and UC row filtering mitigate risks to acceptable level.
- Risk: Residual risk LOW if conditions executed well.
- Decision: Accepted. Conditions below provide necessary controls.

## 5. Consequences & Trade-offs

### Benefits
- **Analytics Velocity:** Non-technical users (benefits managers, clinicians) can explore member data without writing SQL. Estimated 50% reduction in query time.
- **Self-Service Analytics:** Reduces dependency on data engineers for routine queries.
- **Adoption:** Enables clinician-facing decision support for member care management.

### Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation | Residual Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| User discloses PHI in prompt (e.g., "Show me John Doe's claims") | High | **COND-005-1 & 4:** Audit logging + training. TRB escalates if found. | Low—training + monitoring |
| Genie response reveals low-count cohort (e.g., "Members with rare disease: 1") | Medium | **COND-005-3:** Monthly response sampling; suppress count < 5 | Low—if sampled monthly |
| Unauthorized user queries member data | Medium | UC row filters (COND-005-2) + baseline access controls | Low—standard controls |
| Accidental export of Genie results | Low | Training (COND-005-4); depends on user discipline | Low—training |

### Operational Requirements
- **Monitoring:** Monthly result sampling + quarterly audit log review (TRB workload ~4 hours/month)
- **Support Model:** Databricks support for Genie bugs; Analytics team for user support
- **SLA:** 99.5% uptime; <4hr incident response (standard Databricks GA SLA)

---

## 6. Sunset & Re-review Conditions

**Re-review Triggers:**
1. **Annual:** If monitoring shows low-count suppression failures (>2 instances), or unauthorized prompt disclosures (>1), re-review required.
2. **Immediate:** If Databricks modifies Genie architecture (e.g., adds cloud-based LLM processing), CISO must reassess before continued use.
3. **Policy Change:** If Acme Health Plan adopts new data governance policy (e.g., PHI minimization), TRB will evaluate Genie compliance.

**Sunset Clause:** If conditions cannot be maintained (e.g., Databricks removes audit logging capability), TRB may issue sunset notice with ≤180 day wind-down.

## 7. Compliance & Regulatory Context

**Regulatory Framework:**
- **HIPAA Security Rule § 164.312(a)(2)(i):** Access controls (unique user identification, emergency access procedures, audit logging, encryption/decryption)
- **HIPAA Security Rule § 164.312(b):** Audit controls—audit records and reports on access and other activity

**Acme Health Plan Policies:**
- **POLICY-2024-DG (Data Governance):** Defines PHI handling, retention, and access review requirements
- **POLICY-2024-TRAINING:** Annual HIPAA training mandatory for all users with PHI access
- **POLICY-2024-INCIDENT:** Breach response includes investigation of system logs and audit records

**Audit Trail & Retention:**
