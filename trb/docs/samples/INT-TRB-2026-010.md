# Review Intake Request: Unity Catalog

**Review ID:** TRB-2026-010 · **Feature:** `Unity Catalog@Platform & Governance` · **Databricks phase at submission:** GA
**Requesting business unit:** Enterprise Data Platform · **Submitted:** 2026-04-16 · **Scheduled for:** TRBM-2026-05-05
**Primary reviewer:** Data Governance Lead

## Business need
Enterprise Data Platform submitted UC as the foundational governance layer for the lakehouse. The TRB evaluated whether the metastore design and cross-region limitations meet the payer's multi-workspace governance model.

## Intended use cases
- Enterprise governance
- Cross-workspace lineage
- Unified access control

## Risks identified by the requester
- Metastore region lock-in limits disaster recovery
- Cross-catalog access complexity
- Admin burden for large permission matrices

## Proposed mitigations
- Delta Sharing used for cross-region workloads
- Governance Hub provides centralized policy console
- Role-based access templates for common personas

- Databricks documentation: https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/data-governance/unity-catalog/
- Known limitations (tracker): Metastore is region-scoped; cross-region needs Delta Sharing.
