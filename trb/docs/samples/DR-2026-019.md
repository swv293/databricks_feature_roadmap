# Decision Record DR-2026-019: AI/BI Dashboards

**Status:** Approved · **Approved scope:** PHI · **Decided at:** TRBM-2026-06-16
**Review:** TRB-2026-019 · **Feature:** `AI/BI Dashboards@SQL & BI Analytics` · **Databricks phase at decision:** GA

## Context
Analytics Operations submitted AI/BI Dashboards for internal governance and reporting. TRB evaluated row/column filter enforcement in dashboards and viewer access controls.

## Decision
AI/BI Dashboards are approved for internal governance and reporting with PHI filters. Dashboards must be built on UC tables with row/column masks enabled.

## Risks considered
- Filters not reapplied after refresh
- Viewer impersonation leaks cross-team data
- Cache stale PHI

## Mitigations relied on
- Filters re-evaluated on each refresh
- Viewer access uses UC identities, no impersonation
- Cache TTL set to 15 minutes for PHI dashboards

## Control mappings
- HIPAA-Security-Rule 164.312(a)(2)(i) (Unique user identification): Satisfied
- NIST-800-53-R5 AC-2 (Account Management): Satisfied
- HITRUST-CSF 08.b (Audit and Accountability): Satisfied
