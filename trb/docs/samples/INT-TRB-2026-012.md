# Review Intake Request: Salesforce

**Review ID:** TRB-2026-012 · **Feature:** `Salesforce@Ingestion` · **Databricks phase at submission:** GA
**Requesting business unit:** Member Experience · **Submitted:** 2026-09-17 · **Scheduled for:** TRBM-2026-10-06
**Primary reviewer:** Chief Privacy Officer

## Business need
Member Experience submitted the Salesforce connector (Lakeflow Connect) to sync Health Cloud and Service Cloud member and case data for a 360-degree member view. TRB is reviewing OAuth scope and PII field mapping to ensure minimum-necessary extraction.

## Intended use cases
- Member 360 golden record
- Care team CRM sync
- Case management analytics

## Risks identified by the requester
- Overly broad OAuth scope pulls unnecessary PII
- Case comments may contain sensitive clinical notes
- CRM deletion/archival timing vs. lakehouse retention

## Proposed mitigations
- OAuth scopes limited to standard Health Cloud objects
- PII field mapping reviewed per data dictionary
- Case comment fields excluded from initial sync
