## Key Terms & Restrictions

### Use Scope
- **Authorized Users:** Analytics team members (list provided: [5-10 names]) with Data Analyst role or higher
- **Data:** PHI-containing member tables (members, claims, coverage) are approved. **NOT approved:** Provider/employee data, financial internal data, or external third-party data
- **Geography:** Data in AWS US-East region only (no cross-region sharing without separate approval)
- **Query Frequency:** Ad-hoc analytics queries; no scheduled automated exports allowed

### Compliance Obligations
- **Audit Logging:** All queries automatically logged. TRB has read-only access to audit tables.
- **No Data Export:** Genie results must remain in Databricks workspace. Users cannot export raw result sets to files, email, or external systems. Aggregate reports/dashboards may be distributed after review.
- **User Training:** Annual HIPAA + Databricks security training required for all Genie users. Failure to recertify suspends access until training completed.
- **Incident Reporting:** Any suspected data breach or unauthorized access must be reported to [CISO] within 1 hour.

### Monitoring & Re-review
- **Monthly Audit:** TRB will sample 10 random Genie queries and review result sets for privacy concerns (low-count suppression). Results shared with Analytics Manager monthly.
- **Quarterly Report:** Combined audit report sent to CISO + CPO (anomalies, any escalations, trend analysis).
- **Annual Re-review:** TRB will re-assess Genie use annually (July 2027). If monitoring reveals issues (e.g., >2 low-count failures, >1 unauthorized prompt disclosures), full re-review may be required.
- **Policy Change Trigger:** If Acme Health Plan security policy changes (e.g., new PHI minimization requirements), TRB may require re-assessment.
