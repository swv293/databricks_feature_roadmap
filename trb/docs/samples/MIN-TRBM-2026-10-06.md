# TRB Meeting Minutes: 2026-10-06 (Regular session)

**Attendees:** CISO, Chief Privacy Officer, Enterprise Architect, Platform Engineering Lead, Data Governance Lead, Compliance Officer · **Quorum:** met · **Format:** hybrid

## Decisions and introductions
### TRB-2026-012: Salesforce → introduced, **Under Review**
Member Experience submitted the Salesforce connector (Lakeflow Connect) to sync Health Cloud and Service Cloud member and case data for a 360-degree member view. TRB is reviewing OAuth scope and PII field mapping to ensure minimum-necessary extraction.

Open questions:
- What is the final OAuth scope for member and case objects, and how does it match minimum-necessary?
- Provide 10-row sample of member records to verify PII extraction boundaries.
- How will Salesforce record deletions cascade to the lakehouse, and what retention period applies?

### TRB-2026-017: Lakebase (Postgres OLTP) → introduced, **Under Review**
Member Experience submitted Lakebase (managed Postgres OLTP) for powering member-facing apps with low-latency serving next to the lakehouse. TRB is reviewing backup/recovery, retention, and PHI-at-rest encryption.

Open questions:
- What is the backup retention policy for Lakebase instances handling PHI, and how is it enforced?
- Does CMK encryption cover Postgres storage at rest, and how is key rotation managed?
- What are the recovery RTO and RPO guarantees for Postgres backups?

### TRB-2026-020: AI/BI Genie → introduced, **Under Review**
Analytics Operations submitted AI/BI Genie (natural-language Q&A) as an umbrella review for analyst self-serve access to PHI. Genie One (TRB-2026-005) is an existing approved use case. TRB is evaluating whether UC row/column masks and filters are proven to hold in Genie-generated SQL.

Open questions:
- Provide test results showing UC row/column masks applied in Genie-generated SQL.
- What prompt injection attacks have been tested and mitigated?
- How is Genie query result cache isolated per user, and what is the TTL?

### TRB-2026-022: Model Serving → introduced, **Under Review**
Research & Innovation submitted Model Serving for deploying trained models as low-latency APIs (e.g., real-time risk scoring on claims). TRB is reviewing network isolation and audit logging before scoring PHI.

Open questions:
- Are Model Serving endpoints restricted to private network access via VPC endpoint?
- What inference request/response data is logged, and where is it stored?
- What are the P50/P99 latency targets for risk-scoring APIs, and are they validated?

### TRB-2026-023: AI Functions (SQL) → introduced, **Under Review**
Analytics Operations submitted AI Functions (SQL) for calling foundation models directly from SQL to classify, extract, and summarize at scale. TRB is reviewing which foundation models are approved for PHI-bearing prompts.

Open questions:
- Which foundation models (Anthropic, OpenAI, etc.) are approved for PHI-bearing prompts?
- What is the no-training-data-retention guarantee from each approved provider?
- What input sanitization is applied to prompts before sending to foundation models?

### TRB-2026-024: Clean Rooms → introduced, **Under Review**
Provider Strategy submitted Clean Rooms for joint analytics with provider networks where neither party exposes raw PHI. TRB is reviewing PHI handling in multi-party outputs and approval workflows.

Open questions:
- What de-identification thresholds and rules apply to Clean Room outputs?
- Provide sample output tables and re-identification risk assessment.
- What is the Clean Room approval workflow, and who must sign off?
