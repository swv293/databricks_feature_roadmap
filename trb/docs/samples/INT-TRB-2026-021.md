# Review Intake Request: MLflow

**Review ID:** TRB-2026-021 · **Feature:** `MLflow@AI / ML` · **Databricks phase at submission:** GA
**Requesting business unit:** Research & Innovation · **Submitted:** 2026-05-31 · **Scheduled for:** TRBM-2026-06-16
**Primary reviewer:** Platform Engineering Lead

## Business need
Research & Innovation selected MLflow as the system of record for ML model lifecycle (training, versioning, governance). TRB reviewed experiment tracking, parameter audit, and model promotion controls.

## Intended use cases
- Risk and readmission model training
- Model versioning and governance
- Production model promotion

## Risks identified by the requester
- Training data lineage not captured
- Model artifact storage not versioned
- Parameter drift across training runs

## Proposed mitigations
- MLflow experiments linked to UC tables via lineage
- Model artifacts stored in UC volumes with versioning
- Parameter comparison provided in MLflow UI
