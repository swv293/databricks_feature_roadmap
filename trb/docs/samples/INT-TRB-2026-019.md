# Review Intake Request: AI/BI Dashboards

**Review ID:** TRB-2026-019 · **Feature:** `AI/BI Dashboards@SQL & BI Analytics` · **Databricks phase at submission:** GA
**Requesting business unit:** Analytics Operations · **Submitted:** 2026-05-30 · **Scheduled for:** TRBM-2026-06-16
**Primary reviewer:** Data Governance Lead

## Business need
Analytics Operations submitted AI/BI Dashboards for internal governance and reporting. TRB evaluated row/column filter enforcement in dashboards and viewer access controls.

## Intended use cases
- Executive dashboards with HEDIS/STARS metrics
- Quality-measure tracking
- Member cohort analysis

## Risks identified by the requester
- Filters not reapplied after refresh
- Viewer impersonation leaks cross-team data
- Cache stale PHI

## Proposed mitigations
- Filters re-evaluated on each refresh
- Viewer access uses UC identities, no impersonation
- Cache TTL set to 15 minutes for PHI dashboards
