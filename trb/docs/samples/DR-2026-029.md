# Decision Record DR-2026-029: HIPAA

**Status:** Approved · **Approved scope:** PHI · **Decided at:** TRBM-2026-05-19
**Review:** TRB-2026-029 · **Feature:** `HIPAA@Security & Networking` · **Databricks phase at decision:** GA

## Context
Compliance Officer submitted the HIPAA profile as the specific security and privacy posture for the payer's regulated data. TRB reviewed controls mapping to 45 CFR 164 Privacy and Security Rules.

## Decision
The HIPAA profile is approved as the payer's standard security and privacy posture for PHI. All PHI workspaces must enable the compliance security profile with the HIPAA compliance standard selected, under the signed BAA.

## Risks considered
- Controls incomplete for all HIPAA subparts
- Breach notification workflow not in Databricks
- Workforce training requirements outside platform

## Mitigations relied on
- Controls matrix maps UC features to 45 CFR 164 controls
- Breach procedures documented in payer playbook (out-of-band)
- SSO/SCIM enforces identity provider training compliance

## Control mappings
- HIPAA-Security-Rule 164.308(a)(1)(i) (Security management process): Satisfied
- HIPAA-Security-Rule 164.312(a)(1) (Access controls): Satisfied
- HIPAA-Security-Rule 164.312(b) (Audit controls): Satisfied
