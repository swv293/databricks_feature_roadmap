## Decisions and introductions
### TRB-2026-010: Unity Catalog → **Approved** (scope: PHI)
Presented by Enterprise Data Platform; lead reviewer Data Governance Lead.

UC is approved as the payer's single governance layer over all catalogs and PHI. The metastore region limitation is accepted as tolerable; cross-region PHI access will use Delta Sharing with explicit approvals.

### TRB-2026-011: Audit logs → **Approved** (scope: PHI)
Presented by Information Security; lead reviewer CISO.

Audit logs are approved as the payer's audit source. Retention follows the enterprise records-retention policy (7 years), implemented by the platform team through audit-log delivery and system-table exports; all production workspaces must deliver audit logs.

### TRB-2026-031: SSO / SCIM → **Approved** (scope: PHI)
Presented by Information Security; lead reviewer CISO.

SSO and SCIM are approved and required for enterprise access control. All users must authenticate via Okta SSO. Local accounts are prohibited for PHI workspaces.
