## Feature Information

**Feature Name:** Knowledge Assistant

**Feature Category:** Generative AI & Agents

**Databricks Release Phase:** Beta

**Brief Description:** Knowledge Assistant is a Databricks feature that allows users to upload documents and ask natural-language questions about the document corpus. The system uses LLMs to search and synthesize information. Acme Health Plan is evaluating for internal knowledge base Q&A (e.g., "What are our member eligibility rules?" or "Show me policies on diabetes coverage").

## Use Case and Business Justification

**What problem does this feature solve for Acme Health Plan?**

Currently, Acme Health Plan has extensive documentation scattered across wikis, policy documents, and PDFs (eligibility rules, coverage policies, clinical guidelines, member FAQs). Business teams spend significant time searching for information manually or asking colleagues. Knowledge Assistant could centralize and speed up knowledge discovery, particularly for new team members and during onboarding.

## Known Risks & Questions

**What concerns do you anticipate?**

1. **LLM Data Handling:** Documentation says Knowledge Assistant is "locally run" within Databricks workspace, but we need confirmation that prompts/documents are NOT sent to cloud-based LLMs (e.g., ChatGPT API). If they are, that's a HIPAA concern.

2. **PHI Leakage:** If Knowledge Assistant generates responses that include member identifiers or sensitive information (e.g., "Members with diagnosis X include John Doe, SSN 123-45-6789"), that's a breach risk.

3. **Document Upload:** Who can upload documents? If the wrong documents (real claims, provider credentials) are uploaded, they could be exposed.

4. **Audit Trail:** Can we track who asked what questions and what responses were returned? Important for breach investigation.

**Which security controls are most critical?**
- LLM data containment (confirm no data egress)
- PHI masking in responses (e.g., member ID but not name)
- Upload access control (limit to administrators)
- Audit logging (prompt + response tracking)
- Row-level access control (if member data is searched, only authorized users see results)
