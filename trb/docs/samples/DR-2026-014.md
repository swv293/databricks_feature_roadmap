# Decision Record DR-2026-014: Lakeflow Declarative Pipelines (DLT)

**Status:** Approved · **Approved scope:** PHI · **Decided at:** TRBM-2026-06-02
**Review:** TRB-2026-014 · **Feature:** `Lakeflow Declarative Pipelines (DLT)@Pipelines & Data Engineering` · **Databricks phase at decision:** GA

## Context
Claims Operations proposed DLT as the standard for building medallion (bronze/silver/gold) pipelines. TRB reviewed data-quality expectations, SCD handling, and audit capabilities.

## Decision
DLT is approved as the enterprise standard for building and curating trusted claims and member tables. Expectation failures must be monitored; quarantine tables are part of the standard architecture.

## Risks considered
- Expectation failures hide data-quality issues if quarantine not monitored
- SCD Type 2 complexity for member history
- Pipeline dependencies not visible in lineage

## Mitigations relied on
- Expectations route bad rows to quarantine tables with alerts
- APPLY CHANGES handles SCD Type 2 natively
- DLT lineage integrates with UC governance hub

- HIPAA-Security-Rule 164.312(a)(2)(ii) (Encryption and decryption): Satisfied
- NIST-800-53-R5 SC-28 (Protection of Information at Rest): Satisfied
- HITRUST-CSF 09.c (Change Management): Satisfied
