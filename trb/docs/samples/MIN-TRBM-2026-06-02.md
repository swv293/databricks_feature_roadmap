# TRB Meeting Minutes: 2026-06-02 (Platform baseline review)

**Attendees:** CISO, Chief Privacy Officer, Enterprise Architect, Platform Engineering Lead, Data Governance Lead, Compliance Officer · **Quorum:** met · **Format:** hybrid

## Decisions and introductions
### TRB-2026-013: Auto Loader → **Approved** (scope: PHI)
Presented by Claims Operations; lead reviewer Platform Engineering Lead.

Auto Loader is approved as the standard incremental ingestion path for claims and eligibility. File notification mode is required for high-volume EDI drops; directory listing is acceptable for lower-volume feeds.

### TRB-2026-014: Lakeflow Declarative Pipelines (DLT) → **Approved** (scope: PHI)
Presented by Claims Operations; lead reviewer Platform Engineering Lead.

DLT is approved as the enterprise standard for building and curating trusted claims and member tables. Expectation failures must be monitored; quarantine tables are part of the standard architecture.

### TRB-2026-015: Lakeflow Jobs (Workflows) → **Approved** (scope: PHI)
Presented by Claims Operations; lead reviewer Enterprise Architect.

Workflows is approved as the enterprise standard for all scheduled and multi-step workloads. All production claims, eligibility, and reporting jobs must use Workflows.
